# MeasureSpark SEO Audit

Analyzed: 2026-08-24

## Executive summary

- Health score before implementation: 68/100
- Business type: browser-based measurement and home-project calculators
- Sitemap: 21 canonical pages, all reachable
- No critical crawl or indexation blocker was found.

## Findings and implementation

1. Twelve of fifteen tools lacked distinct explanatory depth. Six priority tools now include direct answers, worked examples, assumptions, limitations, sources, and review dates.
2. About and Privacy contained thin or pre-launch language. They now describe calculation review, boundaries, browser storage, hosting data, and corrections.
3. Homepage H1 now carries the primary measurement-tool intent.
4. Organization, WebSite, WebPage, CollectionPage, and WebApplication graphs now use stable IDs and explicit relationships.
5. All sitemap entries now use the reviewed content date.
6. `llms.txt` now states methodology, review date, source policy, and contact information.
7. A branded icon and 1200×630 social preview image are live.

## Remaining opportunities

- Extend original guides to the remaining six thin calculator pages.
- Expand each category hub with a decision guide and semantic internal links.
- Add cost/package inputs only where the calculator can support the search intent accurately.
- Reassess discovered-not-indexed pages after 7–14 days; the issue is currently too recent to classify as persistent.
