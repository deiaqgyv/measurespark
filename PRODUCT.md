# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MeasureSpark serves English-speaking homeowners, renters, students, makers, and casual DIY users, initially focused on the United States. They arrive with one immediate question about a physical size, screen dimension, or home project and expect a useful answer without registration.

## Product Purpose

Provide accurate, understandable measurement and project calculators that run locally in the browser. Success means people can reach a trustworthy answer quickly, understand the assumptions, and discover the next relevant tool. The site will add localized language versions after the English foundation is validated.

## Brand Personality

Practical, approachable, dependable. The interface should feel like a well-organized home workbench in daylight: ready to use, easy to read, and never intimidating.

## Anti-references

- AI SaaS landing pages with gradients, glowing cards, vague promises, or fake metrics.
- Dense engineering software that assumes specialist knowledge.
- Ad-heavy utility sites that push the tool below the fold or interrupt the task.
- Thin SEO pages that repeat identical copy without a distinct user job.

## Design Principles

1. Put the working tool before explanation.
2. Explain assumptions in ordinary language beside the result.
3. Use one shared source of truth for formulas, units, metadata, and related tools.
4. Keep every core calculation local, fast, and private.
5. Expand by genuine search intent, then localize proven pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, color-independent states, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced motion, scalable text, and plain-language labels and errors.
